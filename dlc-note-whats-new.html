What's New

A few small changes to the Cup
🏠 Home 🏈 Make Picks 📊 Leaderboard 📋 Results 🏆 Honor Roll 📖 Rules & FAQ ⚙️ Admin
From Da Commish • 21 September 2026

Y'all asked for a couple of changes. A way to see how your picks actually did, and some assurance that your picks are, in fact, your picks. Both are now live, along with one or two things I tidied up while I had the hood open.

Here is what you will find the next time you go in to make your picks. It should take about eleven seconds to get used to.

The Changes
1 The site now tells you who you are
Open the picks page and there is a colored bar across the top with your name on it. That is the slot your picks are going into. If it says somebody else's name, there is a button right there to switch.
2 You each get your own front door
I am sending every one of you a personal link. Tap it and you are in as you. No name to choose, no bar to check, no possibility of accidentally gifting ten picks to a relative.
Bookmark it. It is the same link every week for the rest of the season.
3 Just making sure it's you!
The website will ask you, after you make your first pick each week, to confirm it's you. Then it gets out of the way and leaves you alone.
4 There is a Results page now
New tab along the top: 📋 Results. It lays out everybody's picks for the week and shows how each one landed — win, push or loss — with the points attached.
No more staring at the leaderboard trying to reverse-engineer where your two points came from.
One rule worth knowing: a pick stays hidden until its game kicks off. It unlocks at the exact moment it stops being changeable, so nobody can see what you picked before then.
5 A Home button on every page
A small thing. Until now you could wander onto a page and find yourself stranded there like a man who has walked into the wrong room at a party. Fixed.
And one thing that has not changed, but which people keep asking about: there is no submit button. There never was one. Every pick saves the instant you tap it. If you tapped it, it is in. Go and enjoy your Sunday.
Week 2 scores are running a little behind — they will be up shortly, and I suspect one or two of you will wish they had stayed lost. Thirteen seasons and counting.
Cheers, Da Commish
